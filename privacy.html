Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Asita Publisher ("Asita", "the app") is a private tool used by its owner to publish content to the owner's own social media channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from visitors, viewers or other users.

2. Data the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens, granted through each platform's official sign-in screen. With these tokens the app accesses only:

  • YouTube (Google): the connected channel's basic information (channel ID and name) and its playlists, in order to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to the owner's playlists.
  • Facebook: the list of Pages the owner manages and a Page access token, in order to publish posts to those Pages.
  • TikTok: the connected account's basic profile (open ID, display name) and creator settings, in order to upload videos to that account.
  • X: the connected account's basic profile (user ID, username), in order to publish posts and upload media to that account.

The app does not read private messages, comments, contacts, analytics of other people, or any data from accounts other than the owner's.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to publish content that the owner has created and approved, and to confirm that a post was published. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner's user account.
  • Tokens and account data are never sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, YouTube tokens are sent only to Google's APIs).

5. Google API Services

Asita's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Asita uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account or deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings → Business integrations
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions

When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
