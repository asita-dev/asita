Asita Publisher

A private, self-hosted tool that schedules and publishes posts to the owner's own channels on YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and X.

What it does

  • Prepares posts (text, images, short videos) for the owner's channels.
  • Lets the owner review and approve every post before it goes out.
  • Publishes approved posts to the owner's accounts through the official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Facebook Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API, X API).

Channels managed

A small number of educational and cultural content channels owned and operated by the app's owner.

Who can use it

Asita is not a public service. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by the owner to manage their own accounts. No other users can sign up, and it does not access anyone else's accounts or data.

Details on data handling are in the Privacy Policy.
